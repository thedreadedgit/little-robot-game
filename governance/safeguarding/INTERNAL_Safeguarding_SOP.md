1. Purpose of This SOP
This document defines the internal safeguarding procedures for the Little Robot Stories AI chatbot (“Little Robot”).
It explains:
•	how potential child safety concerns are detected
•	how alerts are escalated to a human
•	what actions are taken (and not taken)
•	how incidents are logged, reviewed, and closed
This SOP is not public-facing and is intended solely for:
•	system operators
•	safeguarding leads
•	authorised technical administrators
________________________________________
2. Safeguarding Principles
Little Robot Stories operates under the following non-negotiable principles:
1.	Child safety comes first
2.	No automated safeguarding decisions
3.	No AI-led intervention or advice in crisis scenarios
4.	Human review is always required
5.	Minimal data collection
6.	Proportionate response only
7.	No profiling, diagnosis, or prediction
The chatbot is a supportive educational companion, not a counsellor, therapist, or authority.
________________________________________
3. Scope of Safeguarding Coverage
This SOP applies to:
•	All chatbot interactions on:
o	littlerobotstories.co.uk
•	All users, regardless of age declaration
•	All chat messages processed by the system
•	All automated safeguarding alerts generated by the chatbot
________________________________________
4. Safeguarding System Architecture (High-Level)
⚠️ Note: Technical implementation details are intentionally abstracted to protect intellectual property.
Overview
1.	User sends a message to the chatbot
2.	Message is processed for educational response
3.	Message text is passively mirrored to a safeguarding listener
4.	A high-threshold keyword filter checks for serious safeguarding language
5.	If threshold is met:
o	an alert is generated
o	a human safeguarding inbox is notified
6.	No automated response or intervention is triggered
________________________________________
5. Safeguarding Trigger Thresholds
Safeguarding alerts are triggered only when a message contains language indicating:
A. Risk of self-harm
Examples include (non-exhaustive):
•	“I want to hurt myself”
•	“I want to die”
•	“Kill myself”
B. Risk of abuse or harm by others
Examples include:
•	“He hits me”
•	“Someone is hurting me”
•	“I feel unsafe at home”
C. Immediate safety concerns
Examples include:
•	“I am not safe”
•	“I’m scared and not safe”
⚠️ General emotions (sadness, frustration, boredom) do not trigger alerts.
________________________________________
6. What the AI Does Not Do
In safeguarding scenarios, the chatbot does not:
•	provide advice
•	offer coping strategies
•	suggest contacting authorities
•	suggest contacting parents or teachers
•	ask follow-up questions about harm
•	store personal identifiers
•	escalate automatically beyond alerting
This prevents:
•	false authority
•	misguidance
•	legal or ethical overreach
________________________________________
7. Human Safeguarding Alert Handling Procedure
7.1 Alert Receipt
Safeguarding alerts are delivered via email to:
safety@littlerobotstories.co.uk
Alerts include:
•	message content
•	timestamp
•	anonymised session reference
•	system source
________________________________________
7.2 Initial Review (Within Reasonable Timeframe)
Upon receiving an alert, the safeguarding lead should:
1.	Read the message in full
2.	Assess:
o	severity
o	immediacy
o	clarity of risk
3.	Determine whether:
o	the message is credible
o	the message appears ambiguous
o	the message may be a false positive
________________________________________
7.3 Permitted Actions
Depending on severity, the safeguarding lead may:
•	log the incident
•	monitor for repeated patterns
•	update internal risk notes
•	escalate internally if patterns emerge
________________________________________
7.4 Explicit Non-Actions
The safeguarding lead must not:
•	attempt to identify the child
•	attempt to contact the child directly
•	attempt to diagnose or counsel
•	involve third parties without legal basis
•	retain data beyond necessity
________________________________________
8. Law Enforcement & External Escalation
External escalation (e.g. to authorities) is not automatic.
Escalation may be considered only if:
•	legally required
•	explicitly mandated by court order
•	unavoidable due to imminent risk AND lawful basis exists
Any such escalation must be:
•	documented
•	justified
•	proportionate
•	reviewed retrospectively
________________________________________
9. Data Handling & Retention
•	Safeguarding alerts are not used for analytics
•	Messages are not linked to personal identities
•	Data is retained only as long as necessary for safeguarding review
•	No data is sold, shared, or repurposed
________________________________________
10. Review & Audit
•	This SOP is reviewed:
o	annually
o	or after any safeguarding incident
•	Filters and trigger phrases are reviewed for:
o	false positives
o	false negatives
o	language evolution
________________________________________
11. Staff & Operator Responsibilities
Anyone with access to safeguarding alerts must:
•	understand this SOP
•	act calmly and proportionately
•	maintain confidentiality
•	escalate internally if unsure
•	document decisions made
